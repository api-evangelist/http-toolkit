CORS can be complicated. If you're struggling with it, you might discover the concept of a 'CORS proxy' that promises to solve this, like cors-anywhere or one of the many 'free CORS proxy' hosted services. CORS proxies let you bypass the security restrictions that CORS applies, with just a tiny change of URL.
